Aflow Read

Your books. Your space.

Aflow Read is a free personal reading app for iPhone and iPad. Bring your own EPUB ebooks, PDF documents and CBZ comics, organize your library and enjoy reading without a subscription.

Free to read and organize your own publications. Books and comics are not included. Optional Buy Us a Coffee purchases do not unlock reading functionality.

A library you make your own

Bring your books together.

Add your EPUB, PDF and CBZ files from Files or connect a folder, including nested folders. Organize publications into collections, keep reading progress and bookmarks, and manage hidden or removed books from your library.

EPUB ebooks

Adjust text size, typeface, spacing and appearance. Automatic, Single Page and Facing Pages adapt to the space available.

PDF documents

Read documents with page navigation, zoom, bookmarks and saved reading progress.

CBZ comics

Enjoy comics and manga with zoom, reading direction, flexible page layouts, bookmarks and saved progress.

Room to settle in

From a phone to a larger page.

Designed for iPhone and iPad, with layouts that respond to the available screen space. EPUB and CBZ reading modes adapt to the space available, from a single page to eligible two-page layouts.

Your files remain under your control, with local storage and optional iCloud Drive file management. Keep independent backups. Provider access and download settings still apply. iCloud Drive file management does not automatically synchronize your library or reading state.

Free, with optional support

A coffee is a thank-you.

Reading and organizing your own publications are free. If you’d like to support continued development, Buy Us a Coffee offers optional, one-time consumable purchases through Apple. They are not subscriptions and do not add features. Choose only when you want to; the price is shown in the app.
