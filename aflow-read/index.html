A library you make your own

Bring your books together.

Open EPUBs from Files or connect a folder, including nested folders. Organize books into collections, keep your reading progress and bookmarks, and understand when several linked files represent the same book.

Read your way

Adjust text size, typeface, spacing and appearance. Automatic, Single Page and Facing Pages adapt to the space available.

Find your place

Use Contents, Search, bookmarks and Go Back to move through a book while keeping your reading place.

Enjoy the illustrations

Browse supported EPUB illustrations and jump to their place in the book. Availability depends on how the EPUB is authored.

Room to settle in

From a phone to a larger page.

Designed for iPhone and iPad, with layouts that respond to the available screen space. A narrow view keeps a single readable page; wider layouts can show two pages when your chosen reading mode allows.

Keep your own EPUB files and backups. Files from iCloud Drive or another provider remain subject to that provider’s access and download settings. Aflow’s structured Cloud library sync is not part of the Free launch.

A different kind of page

Space that adapts with Duo.

Move between a compact Closed view and a wider Open view. Aflow Read responds to the available space, while your selected reading mode decides when two pages are appropriate.

These native captures use original demo content in the official Duo simulator. Physical Duo hardware acceptance remains separate.

Free, with optional support

A coffee is a thank-you.

All launch reading features are Free. If you’d like to support continued development, Buy Us a Coffee offers optional, one-time consumable purchases through Apple. They are not subscriptions and do not add features. Choose only when you want to; the price is shown in the app.
