A little help with your library.

Getting started, finding a book, or reconnecting a folder? Start here, or email help@aflowlabs.com.

Contact Aflow Read support

Import EPUBs and connect folders

Use Add Books to choose EPUB files, or Choose Folder to connect an EPUB folder through Files. Nested folders can be discovered without registering each one separately. Only import books you are permitted to use.

If a parent folder includes existing sources, Aflow Read offers Consolidate Library Sources. Cancel leaves the existing configuration unchanged. Consolidating reconciles the parent’s books before retiring redundant child-source registrations, preserving existing logical books and their reading data.

The progress screen shows the current stage. Stop Consolidation asks for confirmation while cancellation is possible. Once Saving Library begins, let saving finish; Done does not silently cancel the operation.

Understand source and Bookshelf counts

Linked EPUB files count source references. Distinct books count the logical books those references represent. Additional copies are references beyond one per logical book. For example, multiple files can refer to one book without creating extra Bookshelf books.

Open source details and choose Show books with multiple files to inspect those relationships. Check for an active search or filter when comparing Bookshelf counts. Different counts alone do not establish that books are missing. Do not delete or merge personal files just to make the numbers match.

Reconnect an unavailable source

  1. Check that the original folder or EPUB still exists in Files.
  2. Make sure the file provider is signed in and the book is downloaded or available.
  3. Return to Aflow Read and refresh the source when access is restored.
  4. If permission has expired or a folder has moved, choose the appropriate folder again through the supported source controls.

Keep your original files and backups. Avoid deleting Aflow Read as a troubleshooting step: that can remove local progress, bookmarks and other reading information.

Make reading comfortable

Open Appearance to adjust text size, fonts, spacing and the reading mode. Single Page stays in one column. Automatic and Facing Pages use available width; a narrow screen can fall back to one column while retaining your preference.

Use Contents, Search, bookmarks and Go Back for navigation. Illustrations shows supported image occurrences from the EPUB. Some vector-only or unusually authored content may not provide an exact gallery target.

If a text-size change is unavailable, describe the book’s layout, orientation and steps you took when contacting us. Please do not email a private EPUB or its text in your first message.

Buy Us a Coffee

A Coffee, A Treat and Big Thanks are optional Apple consumable support purchases. They unlock no additional functionality, are not subscriptions and do not grant Lifetime Pro. You can keep using every Free reading feature without purchasing.

Apple shows the current price and confirms the payment. A pending purchase is not a confirmed payment; you can keep reading while approval is outstanding. If an option is unavailable, check your connection and reload the support options. Do not repeatedly press purchase while Apple is confirming an outcome.

Consumed tips do not restore as a permanent entitlement on another device. They do not need restoration to keep your Free reading access. For billing or refund questions, use Apple’s purchase support. Never email payment-card details or account passwords.

Send a useful support request

In Settings → Send Feedback, you can edit the prepared email before sending. Include the app version, device type, operating-system version and a short description of the steps. There is no automatic book or diagnostic attachment. Send only information you wish to share.

For a visual issue, a screenshot with private details removed may help. Contact us before sending any private book, full library export or diagnostic file.
